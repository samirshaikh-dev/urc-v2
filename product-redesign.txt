## My final recommendation

Keep the entire product experience to three levels:

| Level | Page                 | Purpose                                    |
| ----- | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | All Products         | Discover categories                        |
| 2     | Category pages       | Explore products within a category         |
| 3     | Product detail pages | Specifications, applications and enquiries |

One important SEO recommendation: Give every category its own unique title, meta description, introductory content and product links. Don't duplicate the same category description across pages.

This architecture will keep United Rubber Cork's website minimal while making it scalable as you add more products.
