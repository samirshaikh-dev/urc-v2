
# AMCORK — Target Customer & Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

## 1. Document Purpose

This document defines AMCORK's target customers, ideal customer profiles, industry segments, buyer personas, product-to-industry mapping, customer needs, and website conversion strategy.

It serves as a reference for:
- Website design and UX decisions.
- Homepage messaging and content.
- Product categorization and navigation.
- Industry-specific landing pages.
- SEO and organic search strategy.
- Lead generation and quotation workflows.

All design and content decisions should align with this document.

---

## 2. Business Positioning

### Core Positioning

**Industrial Cork Materials & Customized Component Manufacturing Solutions.**

AMCORK is an industrial cork material manufacturer and supplier serving businesses across India.

The company offers natural cork and rubberised cork products, including customized industrial components manufactured according to customer drawings, samples, dimensions, and requirements.

### Business Profile

| Attribute | Direction |
|---|---|
| Business Model | B2B Manufacturing & Supply |
| Primary Market | India |
| Primary Focus | Industrial Applications |
| Customer Type | OEMs, Manufacturers, Bulk Buyers |
| Secondary Customers | Distributors and Specialized Businesses |
| Sales Model | Bulk Supply + Custom Manufacturing |
| Primary Conversion | Request a Quote |

### Positioning Statement

AMCORK provides industrial cork materials and customized cork components for manufacturers across India, supporting applications in automotive, transformers, machinery, sealing, insulation, and specialized industries.

### What AMCORK Is NOT

Do not position AMCORK as:
- A generic cork retail store.
- A consumer-focused e-commerce business.
- A decorative cork-only brand.
- A company selling unrelated industrial products.

The primary identity must remain industrial manufacturing and B2B supply.

---

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Primary ICP

AMCORK's ideal customers are Indian manufacturers and industrial businesses that:

- Use cork or rubberised cork in their products, assemblies, or manufacturing processes.
- Purchase materials or components in bulk or through repeat orders.
- Require standard or customized dimensions, thicknesses, grades, and shapes.
- Need products manufactured according to technical drawings or physical samples.
- Value consistent material quality and dependable supply.
- Want to improve production efficiency and control component costs.
- Have procurement, engineering, production, or quality teams involved in purchasing decisions.

### Primary Customer Categories

1. Transformer and electrical equipment manufacturers.
2. Automotive and auto-component manufacturers.
3. Industrial machinery manufacturers.
4. Gasket and sealing-component fabricators.
5. Industrial material distributors and bulk buyers.

## 6. Buyer Personas

The website must address different people involved in industrial purchasing.

| Persona | Primary Concern | Information Required |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase Manager | Price and availability | Quotation, quantity, delivery |
| Procurement Manager | Supplier reliability | Product range, capabilities |
| Design Engineer | Technical suitability | Dimensions, grade, drawings |
| Mechanical Engineer | Application compatibility | Specifications and product details |
| Electrical Engineer | Transformer applications | Relevant technical information |
| Production Manager | Production efficiency | Custom manufacturing and consistency |
| Quality Manager | Product conformance | Standards, specifications, reports |
| Business Owner | Commercial value | Capabilities and business benefits |
| Distributor | Product range and supply | Available products and bulk pricing |

### Content Strategy by Persona

- Engineers need accurate technical specifications.
- Procurement managers need clear quotation options.
- Production managers need customization information.
- Quality managers need verifiable standards and documentation.
- Business owners need manufacturing credibility and commercial value.

Avoid addressing every audience with the same generic marketing content.

### Claims & Verification Rules

Never invent or publish unsupported claims such as:

- ISO certifications without valid certification documents.
- Guaranteed performance across every application.
- Best or number-one manufacturer.
- Guaranteed percentage savings without evidence.
- Customer names or logos without permission.
- Unverified production capacity.
- Unverified delivery commitments.
- Unverified years of experience.

The existing product information mentions IS 4253 Part I for cork sheets and Part II for rubberised cork sheets. Confirm applicability and documentation before publishing compliance claims.

## 10. Conversion Strategy

### Primary CTA

**Request a Quote**

A quotation-led experience is more appropriate than an e-commerce Add to Cart flow because many industrial products require custom specifications and bulk purchasing.

### Secondary CTAs

- Explore Products.
- Discuss a Custom Requirement.
- Send a Drawing or Sample.
- Download Product Catalogue.
- WhatsApp Enquiry.
- Contact AMCORK.

### Recommended Enquiry Form

- Name.
- Company name.
- Business email or phone.
- Product required.
- Approximate quantity.
- Dimensions and specifications.
- Application or industry.
- Optional drawing or sample upload.

Keep the initial form short. Do not require every technical detail before a buyer can submit an enquiry.

### Product Page Requirements

Each product page should contain:

- Product name.
- Clear product description.
- Actual product image.
- Industrial applications.
- Relevant industries.
- Available sizes and thicknesses.
- Grades and mesh sizes, where applicable.
- Customization options.
- Verified standards and specifications.
- Related products.
- Request a Quote CTA.

## 12. Design & Content Principles

### Visual Direction

AMCORK should look like a credible, modern industrial manufacturer.

- Minimal and professional.
- Industrial and product-focused.
- Strong typography.
- Clear content hierarchy.
- Real product and manufacturing photography.
- Purposeful whitespace.
- Clean layouts.
- Subtle, functional motion.
- Mobile-first responsive design.

### Brand Colours

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Primary | #650510 |
| Secondary | #F58634 |
| Accent | Existing brand yellow |
| Background | #FFFFFF |
| Soft Background | #F7F7F5 |
| Main Text | #171717 |
| Secondary Text | #686868 |

Use maroon for primary actions and brand moments. Use orange and yellow sparingly as accents.

Do not make every section a saturated brand-colour block.

### Content Rules

- Lead with customer applications and benefits.
- Use plain, technically accurate language.
- Make specifications easy to scan.
- Explain customization clearly.
- Avoid exaggerated marketing language.
- Prefer actual AMCORK product and factory photography.
- Never invent specifications, certifications, customer references, or performance claims.
- Keep homepage content concise and move detailed technical information to product pages.
