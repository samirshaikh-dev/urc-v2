## Important UX improvements

- Make the enquiry form easy to complete.
- Use proper email and phone validation.
- Add a dropdown for product selection.
- Show clear success and error messages.
- Make phone and email clickable.
- Ensure the form works properly on mobile.
- Include spam protection.

My recommendation: Don't create separate cards for phone, email, address and business hours. Put them together in one clean contact-information column beside the enquiry form. This will make the page feel much more premium and less template-like.
